Questions of the court

Is Persian Crown really free?

Yes — 100% free, ~5 MB, no pay-to-win walls. Your progress saves to your Gmail and to your device.

Does it work offline?

Fully. The whole campaign, building and battles run offline with up to 12 hours of offline progress. Online mode adds PvP, chat and cloud save.

Which languages are supported?

Persian, English and Arabic — including native numerals and right-to-left layout. Switch anytime from Settings.

How do I install the APK?

Download the APK, allow “install from unknown sources” once, tap the file and open. The game locks to landscape like a true war title.
